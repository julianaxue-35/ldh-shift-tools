LDH Shift Tools

Shift-recording tools: enter animals, work through the exam, export Word & Excel. Full exam notes stay on this phone until you export or share them.

Surgery Notes

Desexing shift — castrate / cryptorchid / spey / pregnant spey / pyometra, dosing, vitals log, post-op.

Dental Chart

Canine / feline tooth chart for dental procedures — findings, probing depths, extractions. Fills the surgery note and downloads a PDF per animal.

Sick & Injured Notes

Ward round shift — AA Vet Flag Log / Vet Reminder Report import, assessment, treatment plan.

Processing Notes

Intake shift — In Care Inventory import, physical exam, pathway and procedures.

Medication Charts

Review medication charts registered from Processing, Sick & Injured, or Surgery, and export a printable 14-day Excel chart per animal.

Shift Ops Dashboard ↗

Live, shared view of flagged cases, shift progress, and disease surveillance across the team — separate sign-in required (not the access code above).

Each tool keeps working offline once loaded. Bookmark it or use "Add to Home Screen" on a phone or tablet for one-tap access. Full exam notes stay on this device until you export (Word/Excel) or use Share/Email. To keep the live dashboard up to date, the tools also send it a short summary for each animal: animal ID, location, shift, whether it is done, and for flagged animals a brief medication, treatment and plan note.
