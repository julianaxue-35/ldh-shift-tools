LDH Shift Tools

Offline shift-recording tools — enter animals, work through the exam, export Word & Excel. Everything is stored only in this browser until you export or share it.

Sick & Injured Notes

Each tool works standalone and offline once loaded — bookmark or "Add to Home Screen" on a phone/tablet for one-tap access. Data stays on the device until you export (Word/Excel) or use Share/Email; nothing is sent anywhere else.
